# Pricing Models and Costs

[← Documentation home](./README)

Extimator supports multiple pricing models and cost adjustments. The goal is to make totals clear and consistent, so your exports are easy to review.

---

## Pricing models

- Time and Materials
- Fixed Price
- Hybrid

Set the model in the Pricing panel. This affects how totals are presented in summaries and exports.

Guidance:
- **Time and Materials:** best when scope is likely to change. Share role hours and ranges.
- **Fixed Price:** best when the scope is stable. Add clear assumptions and risk buffer.
- **Hybrid:** useful when part of the project is fixed (for example discovery) and part is variable.

## Role rates

Each role has an hourly rate. Tasks inherit their cost from assigned roles and hours.

Best practices:
- Define rates per role, not per person.
- Keep rates realistic for your target region and seniority.
- If a role is optional (for example project management), include it as a role with explicit hours so it is visible.

## General costs and risk buffer

General costs add percentage based adjustments. Examples include:
- Risk buffer
- Overhead

These are applied after base effort totals.

How to use risk buffer:
- Apply smaller task-level risk markups to high-uncertainty tasks.
- Use a general risk buffer when uncertainty affects the full project (for example an unclear API or shifting requirements).
