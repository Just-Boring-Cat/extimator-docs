# What is Extimator?

[← Documentation home](./README)

Extimator is a one-page estimation workspace for freelancers, consultants, and small teams. It helps you convert a project scope into a structured estimate with:
- categories and tasks
- roles and hours
- sprint planning and meeting load
- exports (Excel and PDF) for sharing

---

## Who Extimator is for

Extimator is a good fit if you:
- estimate software, design, or consulting projects regularly
- need a clear breakdown of scope, effort, and delivery plan
- want client-ready exports without building spreadsheets from scratch

---

## What Extimator does

- **Structured estimation:** categories, tasks, roles, and hours in one place
- **Real-time totals:** summaries update as you edit tasks and assignments
- **Sprint planning:** compare effort vs planned capacity and meeting load
- **Exports:** create clean Excel and PDF summaries for review and sign-off

---

## What Extimator does not do

- It is not a project management tool (no ticket workflow, boards, or sprint execution).
- It does not track time.
- It is not a full billing or invoicing system.

---

## How people typically use Extimator

- **Client proposals:** turn a vague scope into an estimate with clear assumptions
- **Internal planning:** validate whether the work fits into a target number of sprints
- **Team alignment:** make scope and effort visible before delivery starts
